# Privacy and compliance

Use for personal data, staff/contractor records, documents, analytics,
notifications, exports, support access, retention, or deletion requests.

- Classify data before storing or sending it; collect the minimum fields and
  purpose required for the workflow.
- Mask sensitive values in logs, notifications, screenshots, test fixtures,
  exports, and AI prompts. Never expose secrets or full financial identifiers.
- Define retention and legal-hold behavior for audit, finance, stock, and
  operational records. Prefer reversible/anonymizing deletion over destroying
  mandatory evidence.
- Make user/tenant data export, correction, and deletion scope-checked,
  authenticated, rate-limited, and fully audited.
- Record consent/legal basis where required and ensure sub-processors/storage
  locations are explicit in operational documentation.
