About 400 commits in five weeks, grouped by what a person would look for. Newest first inside each area. The git history has the detail
(`git log --since="5 weeks ago"`); this file says what changed and why, and where to read more. Keep adding to it: one line per change that a

## Dashboards, PAT and sync recovery (27 Nov)

- **27 Nov** Opening stock for go-live (owner; migration `20261229000000_opening_stock_load`, two new tables). Inventory / Stores → Setup → **Opening Stock** (was Initial Stock Setup): download the template (every item with its unit and master cost), fill the counted quantity and unit cost per store, upload it with the scan of the signed count sheet, submit; another person approves (`OpeningStockLoad`, OSL-YYYY-MM-NNNN; `lib/opening-stock-rules.ts` on the screen and in `OpeningStockService`). Approval creates per line a numbered FIFO batch at the line's cost, adds the store stock and writes an `OPENING_BALANCE` ledger entry, in one transaction. An item is loaded once per store (stock or an open load refuses it: corrections are cycle counts). Submit `inventory.master`, approve `inventory.approve`, never the submitter; store scope enforced. The direct set-stock route (`POST /api/inventory/stock`, no second person, logged as a cycle-count correction) is now administrators only. Compliance audit S2 also checks loads approved by their submitter. Guide `opening-stock` on Help. Found before go-live: 34 of 42 items have no master cost. Test: `tests/unit/opening-stock.test.ts`.
- **27 Nov** **Help** page (`/help`, sidebar under Dashboard, `dashboard.read`: every office user) shows the system guides with numbered steps, the real menu path, "You can do this" for the reader's own steps, and screenshots. New guide `purchase` (`src/content/guides/purchase.guide.ts`): material request -> Stores Manager -> OSP Manager -> documents -> PO -> GRN -> voucher -> signatures and documents -> HO receipt -> HO payments -> Vendor Payment Status, one section per person, 12 screenshots of the real screens on ZZG6 test data (`public/guides/purchase/`, taken by `scripts/zz-pf-guide-shots.ts`; a guide section may carry `images`). The Nexus assistant answers from the same guide. Tests: `tests/unit/purchase-guide.test.ts`.
- **27 Nov** Project skill `sl-inventory-compliance-audit` with the read-only script `scripts/inventory-compliance-audit.ts`: 16 checks against LKAS 2 (FIFO, cost, NRV review, damaged goods, invoice tax), ISO 9001 (batch and Issue Note traceability, expiry, count variance over 1%, active vendors) and ISO 27001 (off-hours adjustments, second-person approval, PO/GRN segregation, ledger checksum chain); scores per standard and an NCR table. First run: 93%, one NCR (three open ZZTEST POs with closed test vendors), count variance not yet evidenced (no submitted cycle count).

- **27 Nov** Inventory audit with the MetaFloor supply-chain plugin (invoice 3-way match and cycle-count standards), three fixes: (1) a cycle count took the system quantity when the count was created, and approval added the variance to the stock of that later moment, so an issue or receipt in between was applied twice (100 at creation, 10 issued, 90 counted -> 80); the system quantity is now read when the count is entered; (2) the 3-way match allowed a price 2% above the PO (Rs. 200,000 on a large order); owner: the PO price approved by HO is the ceiling, so an invoice above it is refused when entered, on the GRN (the GRN is not saved; the vendor must invoice at the PO price) and at approval even with a reason; a lower price is accepted; the price tolerance settings are removed (`isAbovePoPrice`, result ABOVE_PO_PRICE); (3) the match was decided only when the invoice was entered; it is run again at approval (the invoice's own quantities taken out of "already billed"), a stale match becomes VARIANCE and needs the finance approver's reason. Production stock, ledger and FIFO checks were clean (no data after the fresh start; `scripts/zz-inv-audit.ts`). Open (low): the invoice tax is not checked against the VAT rate; a GRN invoice spreads its value over the lines by PO value. Test: `tests/unit/inventory-audit-fixes.test.ts`.

- **25 Nov** A signed-in tab never signed out on the server, even with nobody at the computer. Found on production: the page checks `/api/admin/sync-status` every minute; when the 15 minute access token ran out that check got a 401, the page refreshed the session by itself, and the refresh gave a new 30 minute window. So the background polling counted as activity and kept the session alive up to the 12 hour limit. Now the page tells the refresh route how long the person has REALLY been inactive (header `x-idle-seconds`, from the same activity timer as the sign-out warning) and the refresh token ends one idle window after the LAST ACTIVITY, not after the refresh (`refreshLifetimeSeconds(authTime, now, idleSeconds)`); inactive for 30 minutes or more is refused (`SESSION_IDLE`, cookies cleared). A page load or an older browser without the header counts as active. The value comes from the browser, which is the same trust as its sign-out timer. Tests: `tests/unit/session-policy.test.ts`, and an end to end run against localhost (idle 0, 10, 25, 31 minutes).
