SLT-ERP BRIDGE

Data Collection Consent

Before this extension starts working, Chrome Web Store policy requires your explicit consent. Nothing is collected or transmitted until you agree below.

What this extension collects

  • Service order data from SLT Service Portal pages you have open: order number, status, customer name / address / contact, material usage, team assignment, forensic audit photo status, and BOM (bill of materials) CSV data.
  • Your SLT portal username, used only to attribute synced data to the correct operator.
  • Your SLT portal session cookie (PHPSESSID) from serviceportal.slt.lk / ishamp.slt.lk, so the ERP can validate the portal session and resume sync.

How it is used

  • Transmitted over HTTPS only to your organization's SLTS ERP (sltserp.vercel.app) to synchronize service orders.
  • Never sold, rented, or shared with third parties, advertisers, or analytics services.
  • Never used for any purpose unrelated to SLT-portal to ERP synchronization.

Your control

  • Declining disables all data collection — the extension stays installed but inert.
  • You can withdraw consent anytime from the extension popup (Settings tab), which immediately stops all collection and transmission.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes all locally stored data automatically.
Full privacy policy: sltserp.vercel.app/privacy
